Scope and limitations

  • Seven invented support cases are a smoke evaluation, not a representative quality benchmark.
  • The 63 hosted calls repeat seven cases; they are not 63 independent examples.
  • JEV is evaluated retrospectively against preserved historical data; fresh evaluation validity does not renew original operational evidence or authorize any tool or workflow action.
  • Business priority is compared separately from the original explanation; JEV does not replace the required reason field. Hosted explanations remain in the source and this report.
  • Raw classifier choice and confidence/eligibility gate acceptance are reported separately. Abstention is not counted as a correct automatic replacement.
  • Next-step states have a deterministic rule baseline. A classifier adds no demonstrated value for these explicit Boolean states.
  • Local NLI input-pair tokens and hosted generative tokens have different meanings and are not interchangeable. Cost and energy savings are unknown.
  • Historical hosted latency and present local latency are not a controlled speed benchmark. Cold worker/model loading is included in the first end-to-end call only.
  • No JEV choice is automatically deployed, activated in a workflow, or treated as validated for unsampled inputs.
